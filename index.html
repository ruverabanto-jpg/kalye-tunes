01 // The fight behind the festival

Ang kalye
ay para sa masa.

Kalye Tunes is a concert-for-a-cause. The fight is the reason the night exists: for the Toboso 19, for Pook Amorsolo, and for every community told to make way.

We stand with the families of Alyssa Alano, Maureen Santuyo, Errol Wendel, and RJ Ledesma, killed by the AFP. Hindi ito aksidente. We stand with the residents of Pook Amorsolo against demolition. The music is loud so the calls for justice can be louder.

Co-presenter

Kalipunan ng Damayang Mahihirap

Kadamay

Founded on November 7, 1998, Kadamay is the largest alliance of urban poor organizations in the Philippines — the militant center of the Filipino urban poor. It organizes a long-term struggle to eradicate poverty and build a just, free, and prosperous society.

Its fight is concrete: land and housing, jobs and livelihood, basic social services, and democratic rights. Collective action and militant action are how the urban poor win.

Housing & land Jobs & livelihood Social services Democratic rights

Co-presenter

Kalye Tunes 2026 · UP Fair Wednesday

SIKAD

SIKAD co-presents Kalye Tunes: UP Fair Wednesday. Its mark — two figures advancing together, one with a raised fist — carries the call that runs through the night: collective action, cultural resistance, and art in service of the people.

The name points forward. SIKAD is the push, the pedal, the refusal to be left behind. In the Kalye Tunes coalition, that push is cultural and political — a commitment to stand with the urban poor and the fight for a just, free, and prosperous society.

Pre-event reminders

Bago pumasok.

  • [+]Gates open at 3PM; enter through the main entrance facing the College of Education.
  • [+]10 entry lanes: 3 UP, 5 non-UP, 1 week-long/complimentary, 1 priority lane (PWD, senior citizen, etc.).
  • [+]Entry gates close at 12MN. Re-entry closes at 10PM.
  • [+]Re-entry needs a stamp on the back of your left hand plus your ticket. No stamp, no ticket, no re-entry.
  • [+]Exit through the main exit facing the College of Law; keep your stamp and ticket marks intact.
  • [+]Bags are searched at the gate. If your ticket won't scan, go to the HelixPay Helpdesk Booth beside the main entrance.
  • [+]Lost & found is at the booth near the entrance. UP RCY medical booths are near the Service Gate.
  • [+]Parking is limited; no parking around the Student Union Building or the academic oval.
  • [+]If evacuation is needed: main entrance toward the AS parking lot, or main exit toward College of Law parking. During an earthquake, stay inside the fairgrounds away from carnival rides.

Prohibited items

Mga bawal dalhin.

Confiscated items are treated as trash. Leave these at home so the line — and the night — keeps moving.

Substances

Narcotics or prohibited drugs · alcoholic beverages · cigarettes · e-cigarettes and vapes · perfumes and cologne (other bottled/spray containers) · over-the-counter or prescription medications without a valid prescription.

Weapons & tools

Weapons of any kind · explosives or pyrotechnics (including lighters and flammables) · industrial metal chains and wallet chains · big buckled or spiked belts · flagpoles, sticks, batons · tear gas or pepper spray · slingshots, whips, makeshift weapons · any sharp objects.

Other materials

Large or offensive signs over A4 (flags, banners, etc.) · drones or remote-controlled devices · metal cutlery (except disposables) · bulky objects such as coolers, baskets, and containers · outside food and beverages · backpacks and divisoria bags.

Also prohibited

Cans · glass or plastic bottles · insulated tumblers and reusable jugs over 40oz · noise devices · tents · laser devices and flashlights · portable seating · wheeled devices · animals (except service animals with documentation) · liquid or powder makeup over 100ml · camera accessories (selfie sticks, tripods, monopods, gimbals) · sports equipment.

FAQ

From the official announcements

When and where is Kalye Tunes 2026?

Wednesday, April 15, 2026 at the UP Diliman Sunken Garden, Quezon City. Ticket booth opens at 10AM, gates at 3PM, and the event runs until 2AM.

How much are the tickets?

UP Student tickets are PHP 250 and Non-UP Student tickets are PHP 400, as officially announced. Buy online through kalyetunes.helixpay.ph or at the walk-in booth on April 15.

Are walk-in tickets available?

Yes — the page confirmed that walk-in tickets are still available on April 15 at the Sunken Garden. Arrive early; the ticket booth opens at 10AM.

What time do the gates close?

Entry gates close at 12MN. Re-entry gates close at 10PM. Plan your arrival and any exits accordingly.

Where can I get merch?

The page posts merch links, including a ticket + merch bundle. Check the Linktree or the latest merch post on facebook.com/kalyetunes.

Who is behind Kalye Tunes?

Kalye Tunes: UP Fair Wednesday is presented by EMC² Fraternity, co-presented by Kadamay and SIKAD, with partners and orgs listed on the page.
